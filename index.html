Curves approximate NOAA 2022 Interagency Technical Report scenarios (Sweet et al.), adjusted for the Virginia Key tide gauge and rebased to 2026. Inundation is a simple bathtub fill against the Esri World Elevation surface: it ignores seawalls, pumps and drainage connectivity, so treat it as a screening visual, not a flood map. Storm surge is applied as a uniform peak height. Real surge varies along the coast and fades inland, so this overstates inland flooding; see the NHC’s SLOSH-based surge risk maps. *Water level is above local mean sea level.
